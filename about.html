About Rank Master

Rank Master was built with one goal in mind: to give teachers back their time. We understand that ranking students manually is one of the most tedious parts of the job — and it's also one of the most important. Accurate rankings help teachers identify struggling students, reward progress, and communicate clearly with parents.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make student performance tracking simple, fast, and reliable for every teacher. We believe that technology should reduce workload, not add to it. That's why Rank Master focuses on doing one thing extremely well: automating the ranking process so teachers can focus on teaching.

How It Works

Teachers log in securely using their phone number or email address. Once inside the dashboard, they can enter student scores from assessments, quizzes, or exams. Rank Master instantly calculates rankings and generates progress reports. Teachers can view individual student trends, class averages, and overall performance at any time.

Built for All Teachers

Whether you teach at a primary school, a secondary school, or work as a private tutor, Rank Master is designed to fit your workflow. The platform is accessible on any device with a web browser — no installation required.

Our Commitment

We are committed to keeping Rank Master secure, private, and easy to use. Student data is handled responsibly, and access is restricted to authenticated teachers only. We continue to improve the platform based on feedback from educators.
