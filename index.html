Rank Master — Automated Student Ranking for Teachers

Rank Master is a web-based platform built for teachers who want to spend less time on paperwork and more time teaching. Our platform automatically tracks student performance and generates accurate rankings, giving you clear insights into your classroom at a glance.

Teacher Login

Why Teachers Use Rank Master

Manual ranking is time-consuming and error-prone. Teachers often spend hours at the end of each term calculating averages, sorting students, and preparing reports. Rank Master eliminates that work by automating the entire process. Once you enter student scores, the platform instantly calculates rankings and generates progress reports.

Key Features

  • Student Performance Tracking — Record and monitor scores over time.
  • Automated Ranking System — Instant, accurate rankings after every assessment.
  • Real-Time Progress Reports — See how each student is improving week by week.
  • Secure Teacher Login — Sign in via email using Firebase authentication.

Who Is It For?

Rank Master is designed for all teachers — primary school, secondary school, and private tutors. Whether you manage a single class or multiple groups, the platform adapts to your needs and helps you stay organised throughout the academic year.

Get Started

Rank Master is live and free to try. Log in with email to access your teacher dashboard and start tracking student performance today.
